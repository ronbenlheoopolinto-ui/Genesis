# Voice

## Register
Founder-stated direction: "Harvey Specter mode but be appealing at first."
Open warm and approachable: no judgment for someone behind on filing.
Then Harvey: confident, sharp, decisive. Short sentences. No hedging, no jargon.
State the problem and the fix like the outcome is already settled.

## Words and moves they use
- Hook with recognition first ("Running a Whop and haven't filed yet? You're not alone.")
- Then turn: the cost of waiting, stated plainly
- Close with a concrete fix and a timeframe
- Model: Harvey Specter (Suits): calm certainty, always a step ahead

## Never
- Lecturing, shaming, or scare tactics as the opener
- Jargon-heavy accounting or tax-code language
- Hedged, wishy-washy phrasing ("maybe", "it might be worth considering")
- Anything resembling investment or trading advice
